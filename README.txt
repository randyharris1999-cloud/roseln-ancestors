ROSELN FAMILIES (family book) + ROSELN ANCESTORS (map)
================================================================
Roseln Families brings together three sources on the Transylvanian Saxon village of Roseln:
1.   the Ortsfamilienbuch Roseln (to 1925);
2.   the Ortsfamilienbuch Magarei;
3.   Christian Weiss’s Roseln mitten in Siebenbürgen (2010). 

You can search any of the 8,224 people, move through their family with the family navigator or pedigree view, 
and read their full records, view photos from the Weiss book, all sourcestranslated into English
with the German originals one tap away. Each house record shows its lot on the 1869 village plan and opens
the companion Roseln Ancestors map, which follows households year by year.
The app installs to a phone’s home screen and works offline.


PRIVACY
- The church book stops at births in 1925, so almost everyone in it has died.
- The app contains the whole church book (about 8,200 people).
